Quiz-IN Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Quiz-IN is a quiz app by Barath (the "developer"). This policy explains what information the app uses.

Information the app stores

  • Account details: the email address and password you enter when you sign up. The password is stored only as a salted hash, never as plain text.
  • Quiz data: your progress, levels passed, daily-challenge streak, bookmarked questions, answers you got wrong, and statistics such as number of correct answers.
  • Preferences: your language choice (Tamil or English).

Where it is stored

In this version, all of the above is stored only on your own device. It is not sent to any server, and the developer cannot see it. The leaderboard shows only accounts created on the same device.

Sharing

The app does not sell, share or send your information to third parties, and does not use advertising or analytics services in this version.

Deleting your data

Open Profile → Settings → Delete account to permanently erase your account and all quiz data from the device. Uninstalling the app also removes the data.

Children

The app is a general education quiz and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If future versions add online accounts, advertising or purchases, this policy will be updated before those features are released, and the new date shown above.

Contact

Questions: open an issue at github.com/BarathKD/Quiz-In-app/issues.
